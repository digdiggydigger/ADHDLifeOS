Concept C's momentum semantics on Concept B's spine: the tabs are Today, Areas, Inbox and Schedule, so the life area — not the entity type — is the unit you navigate. Tasks and Journal become views inside an area (and stay reachable from Today's header); Inbox and Schedule are the only cross-area surfaces.
What changed from the repo
One accent → two palettes. The web app's #FF5B5B coral painted everything. Now colour carries two jobs, and never both at once.
State palette. Green = closed, plus the two places iOS already owns it — a switch that is on, a permission that is granted. Blue = in motion — selection, sprints, minutes, capture. Orange = at risk, red = overdue or destructive.
Identity palette. Each life area owns a hue, straight off the repo's five domains — Work, Health, Admin, Growth, Hobbies. Rings, chips, journal entries and inbox glyphs all inherit it, so you can read where your week went without reading a word. Each tab takes its own hue too.
Concept B's spine. The life area is now the unit of navigation, not the entity type: Areas is a tab, each area has its own tinted screen with an avatar rail and Tasks / Journal / Captures chips, and only Inbox and Schedule stay global. Concept C's momentum lives inside it — every area screen carries its own ring, and closure is still the thing being counted.
Nudges → Schedule. B's single timeline absorbs the repo's reminders and nudges, each entry edged in its area's colour, with the rituals list and their streaks underneath.
Totals → rates. HomeView's counts per life area become completion rings. Same inputs, read as movement.
Effort is real. The chips are focusMinutesTarget, already on every seeded task — no new field needed.
Space Mono → SF. Mono is kept for system metadata only; tabular numerals carry the instrument-panel feel instead.
Honest counterweight. Nothing counts a miss. Streaks show hits only, and every screen that celebrates also states what is stale.
BOARDThe screens behind the tabs

Modals and detail views, laid out flat so they can be read side by side. Every field the repo's forms hold is still here — the additions are the effort chip and the closure consequence stated on the button.
